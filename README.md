# Vishal Chhaya Palace Celebration

Build a fully animated, mobile-first wedding invitation website using the attached palace video.



1. Opening: the video full screen, paused on its first frame, with a glowing "Tap to Enter" button. On tap, play the video (the doors open), then fade into the site. Start soft instrumental background music on tap, with a mute button in the corner.

2. Hero: the couple's names in elegant gold serif, with a subtle shimmer, over a soft cream background with gold floral borders.

3. Save the Date: a gold scratch card that reveals the wedding date when the guest scratches it with a finger.

4. Countdown: a live timer to the wedding showing days, hours, minutes and seconds.

5. Celebrations: the event schedule as elegant cards (Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception), each with date, time and dress code.

6. Venue: venue name, address and a "Get Directions" button that opens Google Maps.

7. RSVP: a simple form with name, number of guests and attending yes/no.



Style: royal Indian luxury, cream and gold with deep maroon accents, serif headings, smooth fade and slide-up animations on scroll, fully responsive on phones.



Ask me for these details before building: couple's names, wedding date, each event's date and time, and the venue address.
